# Track p03

**What it is.** A 3:09 lo-fi / boom-bap hip-hop instrumental. The tracker gives a steady 150 BPM, but the snare lands on beat 3 of every tracked bar, so it feels like ~75 BPM in 4/4 (half-time backbeat). It's built from sub-heavy bass (long A1/E2 notes, CLAP "808 bass"), a drum-machine kit and a plucky keys layer that arpeggiates the chords. It centres on A minor, with E minor as the second pole.

**How it develops.**
- 0:00–0:13 (A1): drumless intro. Keys and bass play Am – C→G – Am – Em through a low-pass filter (centroid ~400 Hz) that opens in bars 7–8.
- 0:13–0:38 (B1–B3): drums drop in on an Am–G loop, then Em–Am.
- 0:39–1:03 (C1): Am–Em alternating with a Gadd9/A colour (G triad over an A bass).
- 1:03–1:17 (D1): a darker detour: G – Bm – C – Bm – F – Bm, with the bass on F1 under the F bars.
- 1:17–2:02 (E1–F1): Am–G–Em material, then an F#m → F → Em chromatic slide (bars 71–73).
- 2:02–2:15 (A2): breakdown. Drums thin to one hit per bar plus quarter-note hats, over A – Em – Fmaj7 – Em – Fmaj7 – G.
- 2:15–3:06: the C, D, E and F blocks return in order, and E2 ends on the same slide.
- 3:06: a hard stop, from −10.7 to −30 dB in one bar.

**What's striking.** There's more harmonic movement than a typical beat loop: 9 distinct chords, and only 31% of bars repeat the chord from 8 bars earlier. The Bm/F detour and the F#m–F–Em descent (bars 71–73, 111–113) add unease against the Am–G home loop. The filtered intro is a neat genre gesture. The drums are tight (sd 11.6 ms), and the kick moves between beat 1 and the "and" of 1 on alternate bars, which gives a nodding bounce.

**What's weak or questionable.** The dynamics are flat (LRA 4.4 LU; most bars sit at −10 to −13 dB), and only the A2 breakdown changes the energy. There's no singable lead: the "melody" is the keys' arpeggio (50% leaps). The second half largely replays the first. The ending just stops. The master is hot (−10.9 LUFS, true peak +1.1 dBTP, some clipping).

**Style tags (CLAP, weak evidence).** Stand-outs are boom bap, lo-fi hip hop, trip-hop, 808 bass, drum machine, vintage analog and dark. All of these agree with my reading.

**In plain words.** A moody, head-nodding hip-hop beat. It starts muffled, as if heard through a wall, then opens up as the drums thump in. The chords drift between wistful and something a little darker and more uneasy, which keeps it more interesting than a plain loop. But the energy barely changes: apart from one stretch where the drums pull back, it stays at one level and then simply stops. It's good background for late-night studying or driving, with no tune that really grabs you.

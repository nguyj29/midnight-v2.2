The Bbmaj7→Am7 half-step move over a held A is a simple but effective, moody hook. The arrangement uses standard DJ-friendly devices well: a filter-sweep intro, snare-roll fills at section seams (bars 10, 34, 42), a hook section in the middle and a stripped breakdown.

The hook appears only in C1–C2 (33 s), so B2 repeats B1 almost unchanged and the second half has little new material.

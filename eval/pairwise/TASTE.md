# What this listener likes (blinded summary)

Inferred from how they ranked a set of instrumental tracks. No track is named here on purpose.

**Loved (top tier):** music that is **immersive and cool**: moody, melancholy, hypnotic, late-night or
bittersweet, and that pulls you into a sound world. Often there is a beat to nod to, but a calm, drumless swell
can be loved too if it's bittersweet and immersive rather than sunny. The very favourites have a **big,
physical arrival**: a drop after a muffled intro, a band crashing in after a quiet start, a tight groove landing
hard. They also **breathe**: drums-only breaks, drumless floating passages, near-silent drops, slow swells.
Bright is fine when it's *driving* (a euphoric dance drop), not cute. Sad, heavy darkness is loved.

**Middle:** energetic or loop-based tracks that are fun but stay at **one level**: action/game synth rushes,
polished club loops that run one hook for minutes, hip-hop loop beats with little change, a warm live-band
groove, a heroic or melodramatic cinematic build.

**Least liked:** two kinds. **Sweet and pastel**: sunny, cute, gentle-pretty, lullaby or "focus music",
swaying waltz-like loops. And **tense and theatrical**: urgent trailer drama, or sinister, uneasy, relentless
riffing.

**Doesn't matter:** loudness, audio quality, tempo, length, genre labels, whether it has drums, whether there's
a hummable tune (almost nothing here has one), or repetition as such (repetition hurts only when the sound also
stays flat and the mood doesn't pull you in).

**Rule of thumb for a pair:** mood first (immersive/cool/sad > energetic-but-flat > sweet or tense), then
impact (a real arrival), then breathing.
